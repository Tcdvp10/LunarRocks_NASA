A history of discovery

One small step.
Decades of discovery.

Travel through time to see how explorers on the Moon and Mars helped us learn more about our neighbors in space.

1969

Apollo 11 reaches the Moon

People walk on the Moon. The bottom part of their lander stays behind when they leave.

1971

Apollo 15 brings a Moon car

The battery-powered rover helps astronauts travel farther and bring back more Moon rocks.

1997

Sojourner explores Mars

The small rover becomes the first successful wheeled rover to operate on Mars, testing a new way to explore.

2004

Spirit and Opportunity arrive

The twin rovers find evidence that liquid water once changed rocks and landscapes on ancient Mars.

2012

Curiosity lands on Mars

The rover looks for signs that Mars once had places where tiny living things could survive.

2021

Perseverance begins a new chapter

The rover explores an ancient river delta and collects rocks that may one day be studied on Earth.
