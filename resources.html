NASA data & resources

Go deeper with NASA's mission records.

The NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA) is a public catalog of spacecraft. Look up when they launched, what they did, and where they are now.

The Moon

  • Apollo Moon landersThe lower parts of the landers stayed on the Moon when astronauts flew home.
  • Apollo Moon carsThree battery-powered cars helped astronauts travel farther across the Moon.
  • Apollo science packagesInstruments left on the Moon measured moonquakes and other clues.
  • Surveyor 3A robot lander that tested how to land softly on the Moon.

Mars

  • SojournerThe first small robot rover to explore the Martian surface.
  • Spirit & OpportunityTwin rovers that found clues that water once changed Mars.
  • Viking landersEarly NASA landers that sent back pictures and science from Mars.
  • PhoenixA lander that dug into the soil and found water ice.
  • InSightA lander that listened for marsquakes deep inside the planet.
  • Ingenuity helicopterThe first aircraft to fly on Mars; it is now grounded after its blades were damaged.

Other destinations

  • DawnExplored Vesta and Ceres, and remains in orbit around Ceres.
  • NEAR ShoemakerVisited the asteroid Eros and came to rest on its surface.
  • Pioneer 10 & 11Early spacecraft that flew past the giant outer planets and kept going.
  • Kepler Space TelescopeSearched for planets around other stars until it ran out of fuel.
